NATIVE LOCKSTEP GATE: SHARED NUMBER REGISTRY - 1 NOVEMBER 2026

SCOPE
One place for the numbers that parallel branches allocate in the same space and
that must stay unique: the UI replay family bits and wire ops, the UI guard's
claims, and the gate's stop reasons. A branch that takes a new number adds its
line here in the same commit; tests/test_lockstep_registry.py checks the stop
reasons and the family bits against the sources. Integrated on the branch
contrib/ferrite-integrate (smoke 299a8332 plus missions, trade, peer links).

defaults, tools/dev/native_gate_defaults.lua; reports/LOCKSTEP-DEFAULTS-20261101.txt).
'true' arms the first runs' families (mask 63); every other family is armed only
when named in the list. Mirrors: native_gate_ui_replay_core.lua

3. UI GUARD CLAIMS (lockstep_ui_guard.h uiguard_claim / uiguard_unclaim)
Two ways a replay family takes a guard entry over:
  covered  the family has its own hook at or below the entry; the generated
           table names it in 'covered' (ui_guard_inventory.py) and the guard
           does not install that entry while the family is armed. Trade covers
           27 entries (10 Lua globals, 17 FFI setters), the first families theirs.
  claimed  the family captures in the guard's own hook. Dispatch by entry: each
           entry holds at most one family's handler (uiguard_claim_by[index]);
           in a local phase the handler answers first, otherwise (or when it
           declines) the guard's own rule applies. A claimed entry is installed
           whatever the guard's groups say. Claims are made at the family's
           arming, before the install, and refused (the arming fails) when the
           entry is another family's claim (claim_taken) or covered by an armed
           family (claim_covered: two patches at one entry).
  claimers   build/assets  31 FFI entries (uibuild_claims; SetConstructionSequenceFromConstructionMap and
                           CancelConstruction since contrib/ferrite-construct; CancelPlayerInvolvedTradeDeal is a
                           guard entry of group build, refused)
             missions      AbortMission SetActiveMission MissionBriefingInteractButtonClicked StartBriefing StopBriefing
             conversation  CancelConversation UnsuspendConversation SelectDialogOption SelectBackOption (Lua C functions)
             faction       33 FFI entries (uifac_claims): the whole faction group; CreateTradeRule UpdateTradeRule
                           RemoveTradeRule SetPlayerTradeRuleDefault UpdateSupplyOverrides PauseProcessingModule
                           PauseProductionModule SetPlayerTradeLoopCargoReservationSetting (trade group);
                           SetControllableBlacklist SetControllableFightRule SetDefaultResponseToSignalForControllable
                           ResetResponseToSignalForControllable SetDefensibleLoadoutLevel (fleet group)
             foot          GetUp (native_gate_pilot_foot, lockstep_pilot_foot_guard.h; not a family: the GET_UP key in the
                           stock record of slot 1; with native_gate_pilot_foot_slots also of slot k on client k, consumed
                           in slot k's scope by lockstep_pilot_foot_slots.h)
  Not a claim, no bit, no op: native_gate_player_settings_per_slot (lockstep_player_settings.h, default off) changes how the
  faction family runs its eight entity claims in factions mode (slot k's own values; section 5 has its patched entries).

4. STOP REASONS (gate finish(reason); stats.reason, the probe's STOP line)
Codes 1..99 belong to the gate core (lockstep_gate.c, linux/gate_linux.c);
100 and up to opt-in modules. One code, one owner. A module that stops for
several causes names them in its why/status text, not with another code.
A port or mirror of an owner's code under another define (the Linux gate's, the
lookahead's drop) is named on the owner's line; tests/test_lockstep_registry.py
lists them in ALIASES (a module's code) and CORE_PORTS (a core code).
