HOT JOIN: HOW LONG, WHO WAITS, AND WHAT WAS CUT (23 Nov 2026, branch contrib/ferrite-fast-join)

QUESTION
--------
How long does a hot join take, does it stop the session, and can it become quick and seamless (a short stop at the
save, no stop at the handover, as Factorio's joins)?

MEASURED BEFORE (one PC, late joins at T about 6,000, 200 s into a session)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
From the relay's per-tick release times (native-inputs.jsonl) and the joiner's launcher and X4 logs:
  hq166 (20 Nov)  request to save tick 2.1 s | save stop 8.8 s | joiner fetch, overlay, X4 boot and load 72.1 s |
                  catch-up 20.3 s with the session running | handover stop 61.4 s | 165 s in all, 70 s of it stopped
  fj2 (23 Nov)    save stop 8 s; fetch 6 s, overlay 6 s, X4 boot to its start menu 31 s, load and admission 40 s;
                  2,356 ticks behind at the joiner's first step
Saver's stop (fj2, its log): X4's save 3.0 s, the side-table capture 3.9 s, the 192 MB package's write and hash ~1.9 s.
Per-step timing (native_gate_frame_timing, fj2): host and joiner both sim-bound, sim_us 31-32 ms (render slot 10
~1 ms, FrameTick 1.5 ms, permit wait 0.1 ms). A joiner on the same PC steps no faster than the session, so only a
pause can close its gap; skipping presentation on the joiner would gain ~1 ms of ~33 (not done).

CHANGES
-------
1. Relay (tools/dev/native_gate_join.py consider_handover). The joiner's rate over its reports of the last 10 s.
   gap <= --join-handover-gap: handover now, no pause (why=gap).
   rate > session rate x 1.05 and expected arrival within --join-converge-limit (default 600 s): keep catching up,
   past --join-catchup-limit too, no pause.
   not gaining once the rate is known (10 s): handover now (why=not_gaining): every later second only adds the
   session's lead to the pause. Otherwise the catch-up limit (why=limit). The pause stays bounded by --join-max-pause.
   A saver's SAVED record may come up to 30 s after T plus the lead (change 3).
2. Early start (src/lockstep/launcher/hotjoin.py EarlyJoin, tools/build_play_overlay.py --join-early, probe
   X4LAN_JOIN_EARLY_READY). The joiner's overlay is built from the recipe with stand-ins; X4 starts and the save is
   asked for at the same time; the launcher fetches, checks the files against the overlay (check_joiner), stages the
   save as save_009 and the package, and writes native/join-ready.lua {save, package SHA-256}; the probe takes it in
   the start menu (before the gate boots) and loads at once. Default: the save is asked as X4 starts; files that
   come more than 5 s after X4 reached its menu start X4 again (DWELL_MAX_S; see the finding). --menu-wait asks once
   X4 waits in its menu (the whole boot before the save: a few seconds less gap, with that wait); --late-boot keeps
   the old order.
3. Saver (src/native/engine/lockstep_join.h): the package's write and SHA-256 on a worker thread (state writing);
   the probe's SAVED record follows once the file is complete. JOIN status: cap_ms= (engine save/tables/identities/
   gone/fixture/world/build) and write_ms=; the saver logs JOIN_SAVER_TIMES.
4. Transfer (src/lockstep/launcher/joinfiles.py): j<T>.x4join.gz (gzip level 1; 192 MB -> ~30 MB in ~1 s) offered
   beside the raw package; joinfiles.offered() probes a name at offset 2^62 (NO|offset = offered); the joiner takes the
   .gz when offered and checks the unpacked bytes against the raw SHA-256 the relay announced, else the raw file.

FINDINGS: TWO WAYS A JOINER PARTED (BOTH IN THE CENSUS MOTION DOMAIN FROM T+1, THE DIGEST FROM THE HANDOVER)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Every join saved at T >= 6,183 of this test session parted, in any order: the old late-boot order forced to T=6186
   (fjl3), the early start at 6183..6186 (fje7, fje9) and at ~6890 (fje1, fje2, fje4). A join-tick class the package does
   not carry yet, like T=6046 (BCLK) and T=6058 (DEFR); the 20 Nov sweep covered T 6040..6082 only. Pre-existing. Open.
   Object rows: exactly one object's motion differs, each time a ShipMedium (vtable 0x2b82440) created after T and so
   not in the join save: fje4 (T=6894) at 7200 id 0xa07e8 in zone 0x5b4, fjl5 (old order, T=6186) at 6600 id 0xa08a2 in
   0x4495e; every other differing row is nav_raw only (per-process words). Next: what spawns it and which input of
   its spawn (or first flight) differs on the joiner.
2. Below 6,183: all six joins whose X4 loaded on arrival in its start menu stayed equal (fjl2 6095, fje3 6114, fje10 6120,
   fje6 6123, fje5 6135, fje8 6178), and of two early starts that waited about 25 s in the menu first, fjd1 (T=6140)
   parted and fjm1 (T=6154) did not. Suggestive, not proven; so the default never lets X4 wait in its menu.

NOT DONE / OPEN
---------------
- A joiner on its own PC that steps faster than the session: the no-pause path is unit-tested only
  (tests/test_native_gate_join.py: gaining, not gaining, too slow for the converge limit, converge limit 0).
- The saver's stop (~9-10 s). fje6's JOIN_SAVER_TIMES: X4's save 3.5 s, side tables 0.2 s, identities 0.1 s, gone and
  fixture 0.02 s, the world side table 5.1 s, the package build 1.4 s, write and hash 0.6 s on the worker. fje7's
  cap_world (the world side table's slowest parts, ms): objects 807, srcs 314, lstn 289, sorts 247, attk 225, livef 156,
  viewf 153, kids 145; the other ~48 sections share the remaining ~2.7 s. No single hot spot: the next cut is running the
  independent sections in parallel inside the frame (the world is still then).
- The package build on the worker thread (tried, reverted): both such joiners parted (fje7 at T=6186, fjw2 at T=6147
  with no menu wait, where every join with the build in the frame stayed equal). The build's 192 MB of growing
  allocations on the process heap while the session steps on are the suspect: work that runs alongside the steps must
  not allocate on the game's heap. The write and hash on the worker (no allocations) stayed equal in every run.
- The join-tick class from T=6,183 (finding 1) and the menu wait (finding 2).
- Kit/server flow: the master server (netplay) stores and serves j<T>.x4join.gz; serve.py and the saver's upload in
  lobby.py still carry the raw package only.
