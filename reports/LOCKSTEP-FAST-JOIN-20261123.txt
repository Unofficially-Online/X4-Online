HOT JOIN: HOW LONG, WHO WAITS, AND WHAT WAS CUT (23 Nov 2026, branch contrib/ferrite-fast-join)

QUESTION
--------
How long does a hot join take, does it stop the session, and can it become quick and seamless (a short stop at the
save, no stop at the handover, as Factorio's joins)?

MEASURED BEFORE (one PC, late joins at T about 6,000, 200 s into a session)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
From the relay's per-tick release times (native-inputs.jsonl) and the joiner's launcher and X4 logs:
  hq166 (20 Nov)  request to save tick 2.1 s | save stop 8.8 s | joiner fetch, overlay, X4 boot and load 72.1 s |
                  catch-up 20.3 s with the session running | handover stop 61.4 s | 165 s in all, 70 s of it stopped
  fj2 (23 Nov)    save stop 8 s; fetch 6 s, overlay 6 s, X4 boot to its start menu 31 s, load and admission 40 s;
                  2,356 ticks behind at the joiner's first step
Saver's stop (fj2, its log): X4's save 3.0 s, the side-table capture 3.9 s, the 192 MB package's write and hash ~1.9 s.
Per-step timing (native_gate_frame_timing, fj2): host and joiner both sim-bound, sim_us 31-32 ms (render slot 10
~1 ms, FrameTick 1.5 ms, permit wait 0.1 ms). A joiner on the same PC steps no faster than the session, so only a
pause can close its gap; skipping presentation on the joiner would gain ~1 ms of ~33 (not done).

CHANGES
-------
1. Relay (tools/dev/native_gate_join.py consider_handover). The joiner's rate over its reports of the last 10 s.
   gap <= --join-handover-gap: handover now, no pause (why=gap).
   rate > session rate x 1.05 and expected arrival within --join-converge-limit (default 600 s): keep catching up,
   past --join-catchup-limit too, no pause.
   not gaining once the rate is known (10 s): handover now (why=not_gaining): every later second only adds the
   session's lead to the pause. Otherwise the catch-up limit (why=limit). The pause stays bounded by --join-max-pause.
   A saver's SAVED record may come up to 30 s after T plus the lead (change 3).
2. Early start (src/lockstep/launcher/hotjoin.py EarlyJoin, tools/build_play_overlay.py --join-early, probe
   X4LAN_JOIN_EARLY_READY). The joiner's overlay is built from the recipe with stand-ins; X4 starts and the save is
   asked for at the same time; the launcher fetches, checks the files against the overlay (check_joiner), stages the
   save as save_009 and the package, and writes native/join-ready.lua {save, package SHA-256}; the probe takes it in
   the start menu (before the gate boots) and loads at once. Default: the save is asked once X4 waits in its start
   menu (the whole boot before the save: the smallest gap, so the shortest handover stop); --ask-first asks as X4
   starts (the joiner in ~10 s sooner, a few seconds more gap); --late-boot keeps the old order.
3. Saver (src/native/engine/lockstep_join.h): the package's build, write and SHA-256 on a worker thread (state
   writing; the tables it builds from are its own); the probe's SAVED record follows once the file is complete. JOIN status: cap_ms= (engine save/tables/identities/
   gone/fixture/world/build) and write_ms=; the saver logs JOIN_SAVER_TIMES.
4. Transfer (src/lockstep/launcher/joinfiles.py): j<T>.x4join.gz (gzip level 1; 192 MB -> ~30 MB in ~1 s) offered
   beside the raw package; joinfiles.offered() probes a name at offset 2^62 (NO|offset = offered); the joiner takes the
   .gz when offered and checks the unpacked bytes against the raw SHA-256 the relay announced, else the raw file.

FINDING: JOINS AT SOME TICKS PART: A LOW-ATTENTION SHIP'S LINEAR MOVEMENT CONTROLLER IS NOT CARRIED (PRE-EXISTING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every parting join in these tests parted by its join tick T, in any order and with any of the variants tried: at T=6140
and 6147 (with and without a menu wait, with the package built in the frame or on the worker: fjd1, fjw2, fjx3, fjx5),
at T >= 6183 (the old late-boot order too: fjl3, fjl5, fjx1, fje7, fje9) and at T ~6890 (fje1, fje2, fje4). Joins at
6095..6135, 6154 and 6173..6178 stayed equal. Each time exactly one object's census motion differs from T+1 on, a
ShipMedium (vtable 0x2b82440) that flies in low attention: GCC-006 (Xenon M solid miner, ID 0xa08a2, T=6186),
WIR-567 (Boron M gunboat, 0x8ee88, T=6147), TZG-441 (Xenon M solid miner, 0xa07e8, T=6894); every other differing row
is nav_raw only (per-process words). JOIN_WATCH on GCC-006 (fjx4, T+1..T+200): its position stays equal for 200 ticks,
but its movement controller, a linear one (vtable 0x2c3f7c0, the controller bullets use; 0xb0 bytes), differs at
every tick: the session's has words at +0x18 (0x20e0faa2), +0x68 (0x20c9dcbe), +0x78 (0x410d) and +0xa0 (11) and a
list, where the joiner's loaded one has zeros (+0x50/+0x58 a few ulps apart). The package carries that controller's
pose (POSE) but nothing of its plan, so a ship whose linear move is under way at T moves differently once the move
ends. Open: what those fields are (the controller's plan and timing), carrying them in a package section, a sweep.
The first readings (a wait in X4's start menu, the package build on a worker) were wrong: those runs had such a T.
