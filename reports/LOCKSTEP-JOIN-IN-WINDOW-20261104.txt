LOCKSTEP: JOINING INSIDE A RUNNING PILOT WINDOW (plan, coordinator, 4 November 2026)

Why. A hot join (reports/LOCKSTEP-HOTJOIN-20261101.txt) and a rejoin after a drop (roadmap 15, adaptive report 14) work
only when the join's save tick T lies before the pilot window's take-2: a joiner whose T+1 is past it stops at once with
91 join_inside_window (lockstep_pilot_inputs.h), and the probe refuses it as well (lua_state:pilot_window:late). In a real
session the players fly from the first minute, so every useful join, and every rejoin after a drop, is inside the window.
The join package does not carry the pilot modules' state (hot join report 8, item 5a). This plan inventories that state,
sorts it by where it lives, and stages the work so each stage is measured live before the next.

1. INVENTORY (build218; the pilot modules lockstep_pilot_*.h, the UI replay lockstep_ui_*.h)
A. Objects the gate makes in X4's world during the window (are they in X4's save, and with which IDs?):
   - P_k, slots 2..N: a U::Player from X4's factory (lockstep_virtual_players.h vps_make, take-2), every side effect on the
     canonical player put back; one reference, ours.
   - C_k: the PlayerController the ship's take() (ship vtable 1074) creates when P_k is seated (take-1); its vtable is
     swapped for the slot's own copy (counting thunks, routes 6/7/10, scoped entries 14/23/33-36/49/50).
   - P_k's seat links: parent +0x70 = the ship's control room, controlled-object vector +0x308 (ours), control position,
     assist copied from P_1.
   - After a death (lockstep_pilot_death.h): the respawned ship (renewed), the seat moved to it, the retired controllers.
   - On foot (lockstep_pilot_foot_body.h, _foot_slots.h): P_k as a child of its seat's room, its body.
B. Gate tables that feed the simulation from tick to tick (must reach the joiner byte for byte):
   - pilot_ring (records captured before T, applied after it: delay = 1+lookahead ticks), pilot_slots[] (resolved ship,
     last record, driving), pilot_locals (this client's own, not needed on a joiner of another slot).
   - pstk_ring[slot][tick] (stock records in flight), pstk_slot_frames[] (each slot's last frame: tick, hash, record,
     flight), pstk_held_states/ranges (held keys, roadmap D15), pstk_fire_on/fire2_on/fire_tick, pstk_aim_*, the cursor
     replay, pstk_settings, pstk_flight.
   - pslots[k]: store (the slot's PlayerController block, swapped in for its scope), length, first, authority, assist[2],
     agent_mode, no_highway, throttle, seated/released/roomless/walking, post, retired controllers.
   - pview_state/target_replay/target_tick (the replayed soft target and view), pdeath_slots (dying, lost, respawn dues),
     pfslots (walking state), pfbody mask/parked/back, pfire_slots/missiles (guns leased, missile locks), the UI replay
     queues (lockstep_ui_*.h: operations scheduled for later ticks).
C. Local only, rebuilt by arming on the joiner (never carried): hooks and call-site patches, the slots' vtable copies and
   thunks, presentation (camera, HUD, eye), own prediction, local input rings, counters and status text.

2. STAGES (each one live before the next; the hot join session owns the package and the joiner's load)
W1 Measure (diagnostic, default off): a joiner may load a T inside the window (native_gate_join_in_window = true lifts 91
   and the probe's late refusal, never otherwise); the saver writes a PILOT_JOIN row at T (per slot: seated, P_k and C_k
   runtime IDs and created identities, store hash, held keys, ring ticks in flight) and the joiner writes the same row from
   what its load gave it at admission (does the save hold P_k and C_k, under which IDs, with which parents and vtables).
   J1-style run with T inside the window; census at T+1 and the declared digest parts named.
W2 Carry B: a provider 'pilot_state' in the package's LUA section (gate.pilot_join_state / gate.pilot_join_restore: text,
   versioned, every table above with tick-relative fields), restored before T+1; the joiner's arming takes the restored
   rings instead of empty ones.
W3 Rebuild A on the joiner at admission, from what W1 shows: adopt the loaded P_k/C_k where the save holds them (rekeyed to
   the session's IDs), else build them as at take-2/take-1 with the carried stores, seats and vtable copies; then the
   joiner's per-slot modules start in their 'live' stage at T+1 instead of at take.
W4 Live: J1 census at T+1 equal inside the window; then a J2 and a rejoin (rj) with T inside the window, the slots flying.
Each stage keeps the 91 refusal for every case it does not cover yet (fail loudly, never partly applied).

Session b1,b2 of 3 slots (slot 3 absent) at 20 Hz, pilot window from 300, NGJOIN at 600: both savers saved at T=633 inside
the window with the same portable digest (the save path itself is fine inside a window). The save (w1_inspect.py, the
package's IDS section mapping the plan's ships to the save's IDs):
  - two <component class="player">: the real player in slot 1's ship's room (name, lastcontrolled, controlgroup
    pilotcontrol) and P_2 in slot 2's ship's room (connection room, controlgroup pilotcontrol, no name, a ship listener
    'controlstarted', a full player body: traits, spacesuit weapon groups, autopilot, scanner, discovered sectors), with
    its own computer (entity_betty_macro, connection player); slot 2's NPC pilot stays on its post;
  - slot 3 (absent, never seated): its NPC pilot only, as every slot ship before the window (rj1's save: one player).
  - X4's player creation path stores every new player into Universe+0x230 (the player singleton) unconditionally
    (6fad4f/6fad56, releasing the previous one; vps_make puts it back after it builds P_k), and P_2 comes after the real
    player in the file: a joiner's load is expected to leave P_2 as its singleton. W1b measures it (JOIN_IN_WINDOW rows).

5. W1B LIVE: X4 DOES NOT LOAD A SAVE WITH TWO PLAYERS (w1b, build218, 4 November)
J1 with T=633 inside the window (from 300) and native_gate_join_in_window on both sides; the joiner replays on b1 after the
session. The joiner's second load pass: "UniverseClass::Import(): Invalid number of players. GameClass::Load(): Invalid
savegame. Failed to load the universe from save_009"; X4 goes back to the start menu, the probe boots again and the gate
refuses (code 10: a second session in the process). Static [F, 9.00]: Import collects every loaded player into the vector
Universe+0x200 (40-byte entries) and requires exactly one (8dca31: cmp 1, jne 8de119 'Invalid number of players'), then
makes entry 0 the singleton (8dca72: Universe+0x230 = entry 0). In the save, P_2 and its computer are referenced only by
their own elements. P_2 is in the save because the default stack connects every P_k into its cockpit room
(native_gate_pilot_foot_bodies, lockstep_pilot_foot_body.h): a saved component of the room's child list.
Consequences:
  - Any save taken while the bodies are connected is unloadable, a player's own save during a session included (not
    only the join save). The join save, and later X4's own save path, must not see P_k.
  - Patching Import to take more than one player would load P_k through X4's player path (input context requests, the
    singleton, a camera, control start: the log's 'already a ContextRequest ... PLAYERCONTROLLER' at the first pass),
    unlike the session's vps_make, which puts every side effect back. Not taken.
W3, revised: (1) every saver takes every body off its room around GameClass::Save of the join save (pfbody_call: X4's own
3d8420 with no target, held, with the stand-in camera; the same on every client of the SAVE_AT mask, which is every
present client) and connects it again right after, so the save holds the real player only; (2) the package carries the
pilot state (W2) and each P_k's runtime IDs; (3) the joiner rebuilds each P_k at its admission through the session's own
path (vps_make, the seat, the body's connect) with the session's runtime IDs (the hot join's join_ids allocator places
them), restores the stores and tables, and starts the per-slot modules live at T+1. X4's own saves during a session need
the same park around their save (later).

6. W3 PART 1 LIVE: THE BODIES OFF FOR THE SAVE, ONE PLAYER, THE JOINER LOADS (w1p, w1q; builds 219, 221)
The park needs a slot's scope, which opens only on the UI thread (pslot_can_enter: the frame thread): w1p (build219, the
park at the frame thread's join boundary) stopped both clients at T=633 with 155 park_scope, before anything moved. So the
park moved into the body guard (lockstep_pilot_foot_body.h, the pass on the UI thread): the join core's save_at callback
arms T on every client of the session (savers or not; native_gate_join_core.lua, the probe), and T's pass takes every body
off as for a get-up; the guard brings them back PFBODY_SETTLE (24) ticks of everyone seated later, as after any get-up, on
every client the same way. lockstep_join.h needs no change. Native test: test_lockstep_pilot_foot_slots.h (armed only ahead
of the tick, once; T's pass parks, T+1+24 brings it back; the state names slot 2).
w1q (build221; session b1,b2 of 3 slots, window from 300, NGJOIN at 600, T=633; the joiner on b1 afterwards):
  - the session: both clients to 1200 with STOP reason 1, relay pass, no digest mismatch; bodies parked at 633 and back;
  - the save: one player component (the real player); P_2 is not in it;
  - the joiner: X4 loaded the save (no 'Invalid number of players'), the gate's first pilot frame stopped it with 91
    join_inside_window as designed (completed 633), before the probe's T+1 restore;
  - the package's LUA section carries pilot_players for slot 2 (slot 3 absent: none): construct (vps_make, 295) took 2
    shared IDs, 0x16467c (P_2, created 8001273562e1e15e) and 0x16467d (its computer, created 800127c8e4f05e6e); the seat
    (take(), 296) took 1, 0x16469c (no created component: a listener or the controller); the body's first connect took 17
    (0x16469d..0x1646ad), which the park undoes and the guard's reconnect at T+1+24 redoes on every client and a joiner
    alike, so only construct and seat need join_ids_replay on a joiner.
Next (W2/W3 joiner side): replace the joiner's 91 by a rebuild in its first pilot pass (T+1): P_k through vps_make and the
seat with join_ids_replay of the carried IDs and created_live_add of their created identities, the bodies parked with the
guard's state from the package (parked, seated_since), and each per-slot module started live with its carried tables
(rings in flight, stores, held keys, frames, the stock and UI queues).

7. W3 MEASURE: X4'S CONTROL STATE AT T AND ON THE JOINER (w1r, build224; the merged hot join with BODY and Linux batch 1)
The window's start runs over take-2..take+1 (pnat_pass): construct, seat, the controls take (a share of X4's view
counter, the input contexts' requests off), one frame later the check (flight control 0), the native install, the
FIRST_PERSON swap, the stock capture start, and the controls' release. A joiner at T+1 cannot run take and check in one
pass. Measured (join_controls in the status rows; pjoin_note_controls):
  session, T=633's pass:  count=0 requested=1 flight=1 authority=0 owned=0 controller=1 (both clients)
  joiner, its first pass: count=0 requested=1 flight=1 authority=0 owned=0 controller=1
Inside the running window the session has released the controls (owned 0) and X4's own control state is the normal one,
and the joiner's loaded state is the same. So the joiner's resume does not take the controls: at its first pass it needs
the native install (local hooks), the FIRST_PERSON swap, the slots' players and seats (vps_make and take() with
join_ids_replay of the carried IDs and created_live_add), the body guard's state (parked, the kill guard installed), the
stock path's capture start and first apply (the window's take+1 and take+1+delay), and the tables still in flight at T
(the records captured before T and applied after it, pilot_ring and pstk_ring; the slots' stores and held keys; pstk's
flight state). Then the live branch runs from T+1 as on the session.

8. THE JOINER'S RESUME, WORK LIST (W2/W3; each step built, tested offline, then live; a live stop names the next one)

  R2 the body guard: the kill guard installed, parked=1, mask 0, seated_since and back[] from the package (the session

11. R5: THE PER-SLOT MODULES' STATE AT T (lockstep_pilot_join_modules.h; offline, 4 November)
What a joiner inside the window ran without until now, each found in the module's own state (section 8's R5 list):
- Fire: the slots' guns are leased at the window start on every client (MindlessShootController, trigger written each
  tick). A joiner leased anew at T+1; in w1x the save brought every gun with its Mindless (installs 0, replaced_at_lease 0
  on the joiner; installs 4 and 2 on the session at the window start), but a gun firing at T+1 would have made the joiner's
  lease wait while the session held the trigger. Now the lease travels by runtime ID (guns, group, last trigger) and the
  joiner binds it to its own objects; a gun gone or with another controller refuses.
- The UI replay: sections captured before T and staged for an apply tick after T (uirep_ring) are in rows the joiner's
  catch-up does not carry, as the stock ring of R4; they travel the same way (urK_I). The order queue aliases and every
  slot's applied creates (uirep_q_aliases, uirep_q_applied; runtime IDs) decide which index a later order edit takes, so
  they travel too.
- Hit claims: claims staged for an apply tick after T (hit_stage_ring) and the consumed projectile keys (hit_consumed)
  travel; the carried claims go first in their tick (the session staged them from earlier rows).
- Death: each slot's record (counts, ticks, the respawn's state) and slot 1's held keys travel.
Refused by name before anything changes (stop 91 with join_resume=<why>), not carried yet: a slot dead, stranded, in its
suit or respawning at T (death_at_T); a slot ship other than the window's (slot_respawned, a respawn before T); missiles
shooting at T (missiles_at_T); a gun the simulation holds at T (fire_foreign); slot 1 or a slot k on foot at T
(foot_at_T); a module armed on one side only (modules_armed). Also open, not checked: the hit claims' projectile keys of
bullets in flight at T (lockstep_hit_native.h hitn_table: a key is the shooter's ordinal of first casts in the creation
tick; a joiner's first cast of a loaded bullet is at T+1), the hit queue of this client's own claims (empty on a joiner
before its handover), and R6 (the window's Lua state).
The pilot_state text grew (m5 about 4 KB of hex, the consumed keys up to 64 KB): both buffers are 256 KB now; the
package's LUA section takes up to 1 MB.
Offline: test_pilot_join_modules (round trip, the merge order of carried and caught-up claims, three refusals, malformed
entries named). Live: next in-window run.
