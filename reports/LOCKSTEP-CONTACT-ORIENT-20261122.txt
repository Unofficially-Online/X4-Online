LOCKSTEP CONTACT ORIENTATION (native_gate_contact_orient), 22 Nov 2026

1. THE CLASS
Jolt puts the lower BodyID first in every contact pair at five places of X4 9.00: ProcessBodyPair 0x13d5f50 (body 1 the higher
motion type, for equal types the lower BodyID: the narrow phase runs in body 1's space), and ContactConstraintManager's
GetContactsFromCache 0x13e10b0, AddBodyPair 0x13e1b00, AddContactConstraint 0x13e1df0 and OnCCDContactAdded (each swaps the
bodies so that body 1 has the lower BodyID: the contact caches are keyed that way, the constraint is built that way). A
contact's arithmetic is not symmetric in its bodies (the non-penetration part's r1 + u reaches body 2's contact point from body
1's centre of mass), so the same contact solved the other way round gives other impulses. BodyIDs are process-local: a hot
joiner's load hands them out in its own order, and bodies made in one frame take them in heap-address order
(lockstep_body_order.h). The island order (19 Nov) made the solve order portable; which body is body 1 still followed the
BodyIDs. This was the open "ship-on-ship body order by BodyID" item of the hot join.

Measured (hqc2, a late join at T=6052, the two clients read live right after the admission): 59 of 69 shared bodies have
other BodyIDs on the joiner, and 371 of 2,346 body pairs are in the other BodyID order there (110 connection module / small
ship, 40 defence module / small ship, 19 small ship / small ship, 10 small ship / station, ...). Any of those pairs touching
would be oriented one way on the session and the other way on the joiner.

2. THE FIX (src/native/engine/lockstep_contact_orient.h, a default, needs native_gate_world_identities)
Each of the five compares jumps to a stub that saves rax, rcx, rdx, r8-r11 and xmm0-5, calls corient_cf(x, y, place), sets the
carry flag from the answer, replays the displaced instructions and jumps back, so the native jae / cmovae after the compare
take the bodies in a portable order: bodies whose owner has a portable ID by (owner's ID, which record +0x260/+0x268), then
bodies whose owner has none by (owner's runtime ID, record), then ownerless bodies by BodyID as before. Runtime IDs are alike
on every client (the rid digest part). All five places use one order, so the caches stay consistent (keyed by the pair in that
order). Modes: true (on), 'observe' (Jolt's BodyID order kept, the same counts) and 'reverse' (every pair the other way round
from the portable order); the last two are diagnostics. Status CONTACT_ORIENT: calls and flips (portable order not this
process's BodyID order) per place, pairs with a runtime-keyed body and with an ownerless one, the last runtime-keyed owner,
and the agree digest (GetContactsFromCache's calls: tick, pair in portable order, whether this process's BodyIDs agree).
Pins: each place's block. Stop reason 219.

3. EVIDENCE
- C tests (synthetic): the order (portable first, runtime-keyed, ownerless by BodyID), the same answers for other BodyIDs,
  the agree digest, observe and reverse; the generated stubs executed on a fake branch place and a fake cmov place, both
  modes. Native image: the five pins on X4.exe, the stubs near the image, the jumps in, owned, a changed block refused,
  the bytes restored.
- Hot joins (two Windows clients on one PC, the session save of the hq47 rig, rebuilt overlay): hqc1 T=6061 to 9105, hqc2
  T=6052 to 24148, hqc3 T=6032 to 24039 (with the runtime-ID rank): the digest parts equal at every tick from 6600 (before
  6600 only the known first-capture difference of the dead temporary zones). hqc2 showed the owners without a portable ID:
  from tick 12060 one body of nearly every contact was a station's DockArea (runtime 0x68b42, its record not one of the
  owner's two), which fell back to BodyIDs; hqc3 orders it by runtime ID (the same decisions on both clients, flips
  18438/16255/18918 at the cache, body pair and constraint places on both).
- Those DockArea contacts are sensor contacts (the island order sorted no island of two contacts in 18,000 ticks): hqc4, the
  session in observe and the joiner on, oriented ~2,800 contacts differently and stayed equal to 14,006.
- Solid contacts, the battle census (save_009, 30 Hz, 300 ticks, two Windows clients): on (CO1, CO4) and observe (CO2) give
  the same rows (Jolt's BodyIDs already in the portable order there: flips 0, 52 pairs, 5 constraints); reverse (CO3, both
  clients alike, verdict pass) parts from on at tick 108, the first contact of fixture actors 1 and 2 (pose and velocity
  ulps apart, growing; 97 ticks differ). Turning a solid contact round changes the run, so a client whose BodyIDs turn a
  pair round parts there without the fix.
- Python suite: 2,295 tests, only the known Linux-only geteuid error.

4. MIXED PLATFORM, OPEN
- The Linux gate has no twin yet. Every client runs the fix, so tools/build_play_overlay.py --mixed-platform sets
  native_gate_contact_orient=false on the Windows host (MIXED_PLATFORM_WINDOWS_SETS): both sides keep Jolt's BodyID order
  until linux/ has the twin; then it moves to MIXED_PLATFORM_SETS as true.
- Still keyed by BodyIDs, ordering only: the island sort's tie-break (equal keys: 0 seen), the CCD sort's tie-break (equal
  fractions), the order of Jolt's contact-removed callbacks (the cache's hash order) and the broad phase's discovery order
  (X4's contact-added callbacks). A joiner's contact caches are its own at the admission (warm starts of contacts already
  touching at T); not seen.
