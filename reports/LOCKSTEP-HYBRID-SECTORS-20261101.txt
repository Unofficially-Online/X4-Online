LOCKSTEP HYBRID SECTORS (roadmap C9, user request 1 Nov): A SECTOR ONE PLAYER IS ALONE IN, SIMULATED BY THAT PLAYER'S CLIENT
1 November 2026 | branch contrib/ferrite-hybrid (from smoke fc306302) | offline: no X4 launched, no lane tool run

Tags: [F] established in the pinned X4 9.00 binary (or the shipped scripts) by this repository's earlier studies; the module or
report that holds the address is named. No new disassembly was done for this report. [S] our own gate source, read for this
report. [M] measured (the coordinator's attention benchmark attm0..attm3, build163, 1 Nov; quoted, not rerun here). [I] inference

1. SUMMARY
----------
The user's model: a sector S that only player k is in is simulated in full (high attention) only by client k; the other clients
do not pay for it and receive its results; the rest of the universe stays deterministic lockstep and identical everywhere.

What this design concludes:
- The part that makes it cheap is easy and is built (slice H1, default off): attention is already a function of an agreed view
  point set (lockstep_view_points.h [S]); H1 makes that set depend on the object. Client k evaluates S's objects with its own
  ship as a view point, every client evaluates everything outside S without it, and the other clients drop slot k entirely, so
  S runs at low attention there (no physics scene, no stand-in camera, linear flight, scripted combat). Ancestors of S and the
  rest of the cluster get the same levels on every client by construction.
- The part that keeps the outside identical is the hard part, and H1 does not have it. X4 has no authority boundary: S's
  simulation on client k writes into engine-wide state that the outside reads in the same step (section 3). H1 closes the
  channels that are cheap to close (attention of the outside; the slot receipts; the census; the cluster-level mining test),
  and leaves the others open on purpose so the live test can say which ones actually leak in a quiet sector. The most likely
  first leak is the runtime ID allocator (one engine-wide FIFO the owner draws from for S's physics shapes and traffic); the
  next ones are MD reacting to S's attention changes, the per-callback job ordinal and cluster-level regions near the owner.
- Results flow one way only in H1: the owner publishes a record of S's movers each step (digest + a few quantized poses in
  the input blob); every client admits the same record (its receipt is part of the declared state); the other clients keep
  the published poses as proxies and measure how far their low-attention copy drifts from them. Nothing published is
  written into the engine yet: outside code that reads S still reads each client's own copy (section 6, H3/H4).
- Handover (a second player enters S, the owner leaves, an NPC crosses) needs the full state of S, AI and MD script state
  included, at an agreed step on every client: the join/save problem (LOCKSTEP-NONBLOCKING-SAVE-20261101.txt), whose decisive
  question (save -> load equality) is open. H1 stops the gate (187) when a player crosses; NPC crossings are counted.
- Cost/benefit, with the coordinator's numbers [M]: per step X4's own frame (sim_us) is ~29 ms, of which ~24 ms is the
  low-attention universe plus our sync work (U), ~4 ms the player's own bubble and ~1-1.5 ms a second, remote, quiet bubble.
  The most H1 can save per client in that scenario is ~1-1.5 ms per remote sector (3-5%). The hybrid pays off only where
  remote bubbles are expensive (a battle in another player's sector, est. 10 ms or more [I], unmeasured) or numerous (N
  players in N sectors: (N-1) x H per client, e.g. ~10 ms for eight players in quiet sectors [I]). U, the 24 ms every client
  pays however players are spread, is untouched by any hybrid scheme: C10 and the symmetric cuts of the attention study
  (section 6c there: parallel physics scenes, bubble size) remain the larger lever for today's two-to-four-player sessions.

2. THE MODEL (target, of which H1 is the first slice)
-----------------------------------------------------
Ownership: at an agreed tick T, the sector S_k holding pilot slot k's ship becomes owned by client k when no other player is
in it. One owner per sector, one sector per owner.
Every step t, on every client:
  outside the owned sectors   lockstep exactly as today (identical inputs, identical simulation);
  inside S_k on client k      high attention, client k's own simulation (the authoritative one);
  inside S_k elsewhere        a proxy: what client k published, applied identically everywhere.
Client k publishes, as part of its input record for t, what the rest of the world can observe of S_k at t (state of S's
objects as others see it, objects created/destroyed, objects leaving S, trades and money, events and signals that cross
S's boundary). Every client applies the record at t+d (d = 1 + lookahead: a blob of report tick t is admitted at t+1+k;
2 today) to the outside world and to S's proxies. For the outside to stay identical, every read of S from outside must see
the published value of t-d on every client, client k included: client k's outside world must not read its own live S.
That last rule is what the engine cannot do without help (section 3).

3. THE AUTHORITY BOUNDARY: WHAT CROSSES A SECTOR BOUNDARY IN ONE STEP
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Class: (A) already partitioned per owner/object by the gate, (B) can be partitioned symmetrically (a per-sector scope, the
same rule on every client), (C) needs publication (client k's result, applied everywhere), (D) out of reach in the slices
planned here.
3a. Attention and its consumers.
  - Attention of objects outside S: computed from the view points [F, lockstep_view_points.h: slot 703 implementations read
    the player object and camera list; levels combine by maximum]. H1 evaluates outside objects without the owners' ships
    on every client [S] -> (A) in H1. S's ancestors (cluster, galaxy) and the other sectors of S's cluster are outside.
  - Attention change events: SetAttention posts ChangedAttentionEvent (MD sees event_object_changed_attention) and, across
    5/6, ChangedAdjacentZoneAttentionEvent [F, attention study 2]; 33 of 221 MD files use attention (NPC instantiation,
    signal leaks, missions) [F, attention study 3a]. At T the other clients drop S to low attention, client k does not: MD
    cues run on one side only and MD state is global -> (C) or (D). H1 leaves it open (expected leak, invisible to the
    census until it changes a component; section 8).
  - Systems that follow the latched view point list [S, lockstep_region_camera.h, lockstep_view_scenes.h]: physics scenes
    (one per view point sector), stand-in cameras (region fill, mass traffic, effects, highway parameters), shape refresh
    intervals, XS detail, crystal and mining tests. All but mining filter by the object's sector, so the owner's point only
    ever matches inside S (A); mining filters by cluster and now skips the owner's point outside S in H1 [S] (A). The
    stand-in camera in S on client k fills cluster-level regions near it: region content is a child of a cluster-level
    region, not of S [F, lockstep_region_rng.h: the hazard region is a cluster child] -> objects outside S by their parent
    chain, created on one client only -> (B) at best (a region-to-sector assignment) [I]; H1 leaves it open.
3b. Objects moving across the boundary.
  - Parent changes: every object's parent is a pointer at +0x70 [F, census]; a ship leaves S through a gate or accelerator
    (moved into the other sector's zone), a highway (Highway::Move [F, attention study 3c]), a jump or an MD/AI teleport.
    On client k the move is client k's high-attention result, on the others their low-attention copy moves differently or
    not at all -> (C): a departure is an outcome to publish together with the object's full state (motion, orders, AI script
    position, cargo), and an arrival into S hands an outside object to client k. This is handover in small (section 7).
    H1 counts crossings (left=, entered=; the census compares membership) and does not handle them.
  - Weapons and missiles: shots are physical only at high attention and stay in their zone's physics scene; missiles are
    objects and move like ships (above) [F for the attention switch, attention study 3a; I for the rest].
3c. Reads of S from outside, in the same step.
  - AI orders and searches: scripts anywhere hold references to objects in S (attack targets, trade partners, escort
    leaders) and search across sectors (find_* candidate lists, sorted by lockstep_find_order.h [S]) -> (C): such a read must
    see the published state. No hook point covers "any read of an object in S" [I] -> (D) in general.
  - Trade offers and economy: a station's offers are updated by UpdateTradeOffersEvent (Container::UpdateTradeOffers
    0x4079e0 -> economy update 0x431f90) [F, lockstep_event_rng.h]; traders anywhere query them; money is per faction,
    universe-wide. The attention study's finding that production and trade bookkeeping never read attention [I there] means
    a station in S could stay lockstep-simulated on every client, if nothing high-attention-only touches it: S's ships trade
    with it (dock, transfer wares) at times that differ between high and low attention -> the station's cargo and money
    become owner-decided -> (C).
  - MD conditions and missions that name objects in S (kill targets, escort, deliveries): global cue state -> (C)/(D).
  - Gravidar: sector entries hold one group per zone; a scan sees only its own sector [F, lockstep_gravidar_wide.h]; the
    applies set contact sets, live and player view flags (faction knowledge, map) and post event_gravidar_has_scanned into
    the event queue [F, lockstep_ai_schedule.h] -> the flags and events of S's scans are owner-decided -> (C).
  - God and job spawns: the job engine spawns into sectors with the job's own generator state (JobSpawnSource 0xf06650,
    ShipGenerator::Generate 0xc841b0) [F, lockstep_spawn_rng.h]; its quotas count ships, S's included [I] -> (C) for the
    counts, (B) for the spawn itself if the spawn sector is S.
  - Mass traffic: decided per zone above 'visible' near a camera (Zone::Move -> 0x83ef20) [F, attention study 3c]: inside S
    only on client k (A for the decision); the traffic objects take runtime IDs (below).
3d. Engine-wide resources used inside S (not reads of S, but shared counters and streams).
  - Runtime IDs: one FIFO allocator for components, event listeners, connections, physics shapes, movement controllers...
    (AssignNewID 0xcc220, 96 direct callers) [F, lockstep_runtime_ids.h]; the gate keeps the head identical and folds it into
    the declared state (RUNTIME_IDS barrier). Client k allocates for S's high-attention work (shapes on body creation, mass
    traffic, effects not on the local lists) what the others do not -> every later ID differs on client k. (B): a third
    local list for "work done inside an owned sector", chosen by a scope (below). H1 leaves it open: expected first leak.
  - Created identities: a created object's portable ID is keyed by (parent, type, macro) and a per-key ordinal per tick
    [S, lockstep_world_created.h] -> S's creations never shift the keys of creations outside S (A). Objects created inside S
    on one client only are inside S (excluded) as long as their parent is in S.
  - Keyed RNG streams: AI and MD scripts (per owner, context free), AI waits, MD timed/resume, events (per source, event
    class, due time), regions (per region), spawns (per generator state), weapon fire, warp, build, ... [S, the *_rng.h
    modules] -> (A): S's draws on client k do not move any outside stream.
  - The natural sim-thread word (TLS slot0+0x318), reseeded per frame: consumers that are not keyed draw from it in walk
    order (Object::Move above 'nearby' draws three values for random impulses [F, attention study 3b]; the region pass drew
    from it before region_rng) [F, lockstep_region_rng.h] -> S's extra natural draws on client k shift every later natural
    draw in the frame. (B): an S scope saves the word and runs S's subtree on a word of its own. Open in H1.
  - JobThreadManager jobs: keyed by (submit tick, callback, category, per-callback submission ordinal, body) [S,
    lockstep_job_rng.h] -> a job S submits on client k only shifts the ordinal of every later job of the same callback that
    tick -> (B) with a sector component in the key. Open in H1.
  - The event queue (one due-event queue, delivery order by lockstep_event_order.h [S]) and the AI director's schedule
    (lockstep_ai_schedule.h [S]): S's events and wakes are interleaved with the outside's; with keyed delivery the order of
    outside deliveries does not depend on S's [I] (A, to be confirmed by the census).
  - The pathing pass: agents in batches, N full updates per batch (native_gate_path_budget) [S, lockstep_path_budget.h];
    whether low-attention ships keep their agents in the same batches is not known [I]; if not, S changes which outside
    agents get the full update -> (B) (a budget per sector) or (C). Open; PATH_DETERMINISM walk= per client names it.
3e. Player actions: the owner's own inputs (stock piloting record, UI replay) are lockstep inputs applied on every client
  [S]; on the other clients they act on the low-attention copy of the owner's ship (inside S, excluded). (A)
Count: A for attention outside S, created keys, keyed streams, player inputs; B for runtime IDs, the natural word, jobs,
regions near the owner, the pathing budget (all "sector scopes"); C for departures/arrivals, outside reads of S, trades,
Gravidar flags, MD, job quotas; D for "every read of S" in general.

4. WHAT EACH STEP OF THE PLAN ADDS (slices)
-------------------------------------------
H1 (this branch, default off): ownership, the attention split, the H1 boundary (players), the owner record and proxy table,
   the census filter, the private slot receipts. Measures cost, bandwidth and which open channel leaks first.
H2 sector scopes (B): one scope per owned sector, opened on every client wherever S's work runs: the component walk's call
   into S's sector subtree, SetAttention of S's objects (body creation), the mass traffic and region passes of S's zones,
   AI script runs whose context entity is in S, event deliveries to S's listeners, jobs submitted from those. In the scope:
   runtime IDs from a sector-local list, the natural word on a sector word, job ordinals keyed by sector. Each hook point
   exists already in a gate module (walk, event_order delivery, script_rng scopes, job_rng, region pass) [S]; the new part is
   the scope rule. Closes 3d on both sides; the census decides whether it is enough for a quiet sector.
H3 frozen proxies: on the other clients S's objects stop simulating (no Move, no AI, no MD triggers of their own) and take the
   published state each step, so they cannot drift, cross or trade by themselves. Needs per-class suppression in the walk,
   the AI director and the event queue [I: large].
H4 publication of outcomes (C): departures with the object's state, trades and money, destructions and creations, MD/Gravidar
   signals of S. Each is an input op like the UI replay's, applied at t+d on every client, with the outside reading S's proxy
   (the published state) on client k too. The rule "client k's outside never reads its live S" has no engine-wide hook [I]:
   this is where the model meets X4's lack of an authority boundary, and the reason the attention study recommended against
   it. A practical cut: only sectors with nothing outside referencing them (no trade partners, targets, missions), checked by
   the census, which is a scenario restriction, not a general mode.
H5 handover by state transfer (section 7).

5. RECORD FORMAT AND BANDWIDTH
------------------------------
H1 record (input section 'hybrid', kind 0x59 'Y', registry section 5; lockstep_hybrid.h), little endian:
  0 version 1 | 1 flags 0 | 2..5 the state's tick (u32) | 6..13 the sector's saved ID | 14..15 movers (u16) |
  16..23 digest of every mover's quantized entry | 24 entries n (0..P) | n x 36-byte entries:
  portable key (u64) | parent tag (u32, low half of the mixed portable key of its zone) | position in the parent's frame
  (3 x int32, mm) | rotation (3 x int16, x/y/z of the unit quaternion with w >= 0, Q15) | velocity (3 x int16, 1/4 m/s).
  A mover is a Positional member whose movement controller the census already decodes (the NavigationController's pathing
  slot, or the two getter forms) [S, lockstep_world_hash.h]; the record carries exactly the census motion fields, rounded.
  The round robin takes the next P movers by key each step; the record shrinks to the room the blob has left (squeezed=).
The wire today: one blob of at most 240 bytes per slot and step [S, lockstep_wire.h], shared with stock piloting, UI replay,
pilot records, orders, hit claims and octree reports: 7.2 KB/s per slot at 30 steps/s. The H1 record is 25 + 36 n bytes
(n <= 5: 205 bytes), i.e. at most 49 kbit/s, and refreshes each of M movers every M/n steps (100 movers, n = 4: every 25
steps, 0.8 s). The full state (every mover every step) is 25 + 36 M bytes per step [S]: M = 50 / 150 / 400 movers ->
1.8 / 5.4 / 14.4 KB per step -> 0.44 / 1.3 / 3.5 Mbit/s at 30 steps/s per owned sector, before events and before any delta
coding [I; the attention study's estimate was 0.6-4 Mbit/s]. Sent to each of N-1 clients by the relay (fan-out (N-1) x
owners). That does not fit the input wire; it needs a second channel: a relay side stream outside the step's permit (records
tagged with their state tick, applied at t+d; the step waits for them like for inputs), or the peer links of roadmap B6.
H1 measures the size the full state would have (full_bytes= per step, full_kbps in census_hybrid.py) on the real sector.

6. PROXY MODEL
--------------
H1: a proxy table on every client that does not own S: per member, the last published pose and its state tick (proxied=,
published=). The engine is not touched. Status: proxies= (entries applied), unknown= (keys not among this client's members),
parent_mismatch= (the local copy is in another zone), unread=, and the distance between the published position and the local
low-attention copy at apply (err_mean_m=, err_max_m=): the live test's measure of how far low attention drifts from high.
Later, in this order:
  display proxies: when a non-owner's camera is in S, draw S's movers at their published (extrapolated) poses through the
    render layer's override hook (rdisp_override, lockstep_render_display.h [S]): display only, restored every frame, like
    render frames stage 3. Cheap; makes "look at the other player's sector" show the owner's truth.
  frozen proxies (H3): S's objects on the other clients stop simulating and are written to the published state each step.
  Writing a pose into the engine needs the controller's pathing slots and the cached transforms written consistently [I];
  the gate has no such writer today (everything it writes is time, RNG words, ordering and inputs).

7. HANDOVER
-----------
Triggers: a second player's ship enters S; the owner's ship leaves S (gate, highway, jump, death); an owner disconnects; an NPC
crosses the boundary (in either direction).
What every client needs to continue in shared lockstep: S's full state at an agreed step T_h, including what was never
published: AI script instances (label, variables, waits), MD cue instances touching S, orders, cargo, timers, physics bodies,
attention-dependent transition state (station regions, interiors) [F for the existence of these states, attention study 3/4].
Options:
  (a) transfer: client k serializes S at T_h and the others replace their copy. X4's serializer saves the whole game [F,
      nonblocking save 1.1]; there is no per-sector save, and save -> load equality is the open question of D14 (the loader
      re-draws blocked AI waits and MD delays, takes attention and nav deadlines from the loading frames). A partial load
      into a running game does not exist [I]. Feasible only as a whole-game re-sync: pause, client k saves, every other
      client loads that save at T_h, everyone resumes (the join path of D14 with the owner as the server). Seconds to
      minutes per handover with today's load times [I].
  (b) re-seed from the published state: write the published fields into the others' copies at T_h. Covers positions and
      health only; the copies diverged in everything else (orders, AI position, cargo) -> the worlds part after T_h. Not a
      handover [I].
  (c) never hand over: ownership ends only by (a) for everyone. Equivalent to (a).
  NPC crossings need the same per object (motion + AI script state + cargo), i.e. (a) at object granularity, which X4 has no
  serializer for [I].
H1: no handover. A player crossing stops the gate (187: owner_left, other_entered, owner_lost); NPC crossings are counted
(left= by the owner's mover pass and the census rebuild, entered= by the census rebuild) and their effect shows in the census.

8. FAILURE MODES
----------------
- The owner stops, crashes or drops: S has no simulator; the other clients' copies are low-attention and diverged, so the
  session cannot continue S in lockstep -> stop (H1: the relay's timeout, as for any client) or re-sync (7a).
- The owner's S desyncs from what it publishes: impossible to tell from outside; S's truth is whatever client k computes.
  Trust: client k is the authority for S (a cheating owner can publish anything; signed records (B8) authenticate the
  sender, not the content).
- The outside parts (a channel of section 3 leaks): the census detects it at the next capture (outside roots differ); the
  per-tick digest detects a leak that reaches a declared part (RUNTIME_IDS, slot receipts outside S, fixture actors). H1
  declares the outside roots instead of the full roots from T on, so a strict relay stops exactly on an outside difference
  or a shared-counter difference, never on S's own state.
- A record is missing (blob full, a quiet tick in protocol 5, a dropped owner): counted identically on every client
  (missing= in the receipt); the proxies get stale. A record that is not acceptable (wrong slot, sector, shape or tick)
  stops every client alike (record_*), because the admitted blobs are the same everywhere.
- What the census cannot see: MD cue state, AI script state, faction knowledge, money. A leak there shows only when it moves
  a component. The digest's RUNTIME_IDS word sees allocation differences at once.
- The local view of the owner's own world: client k sees its own S at high attention and the outside as everyone does; the
  other clients see S's objects at low attention (wrong positions by err_* meters) until display proxies exist.

9. THE FIRST SLICE H1 (built, default off)
------------------------------------------
Key: native_gate_hybrid = {owners={k,...}, tick=T, poses=P} (owners: pilot slots 1..4 once each; T >= 2; P 0..5, default 4).
Needs native_gate_view_points 'players', native_gate_pilot_slots, native_gate_clients (protocol 3/4 wire); the census filter

Gate (src/native/engine/lockstep_hybrid.h, stop reason 187; hooks in lockstep_view_points.h, lockstep_world_hash.h):
- latch of the first tick >= T: S_k = class 0x58 ancestor of slot k's ship (the same on every client); two owners in one
  sector, a missing ship or a sector without saved ID refuse (the split never starts; stop 187 at the next gate tick);
- every latch from T on: another client's owned slot is no view point; this client's owned slot is, and counts only inside
  its sector (view_evaluate); the latch of T recomputes every former and new view point's chain on every client; then the H1
  boundary check (owner_left, owner_lost, other_entered, sector_lost);
- gate.hybrid_frame after the latch: the member scan at the first frame (identity tables + native find, timed: scan_us=),
  then on the owner the mover pass (digest, round robin, crossings);
- input section 'hybrid' (C container, Lua and Python mirrors, kind 0x59, packed last; registry section 5);
- apply: acceptance rules, the receipt HYB_1|tick|records|entries|missing|fold (or refused|why), proxies;
- census: after each item's hashes (world_hash_after_item), outside roots / inside root; gate.hybrid_census gives
  HYBRID_OUTSIDE_1:tick:count:r0:r1:r2:r3, the probe declares it in place of the full roots from T on, and the member
  table is rebuilt from the capture;
- private slots: PSTOCK flight state (|Sk:..:h), pilot fire fold and missile part (h), PILOT_SHIPS rows (/hybrid) leave the
  owners' ships out from T on; a battle fixture actor inside an owned sector is declared as /hybrid;
- region camera: the cluster-level mining test skips the owner's point outside its sector (rc_shape_own).
Probe (tools/dev/native_gate_probe.lua): validation at load, arming after view points (HYBRID_ARMED), the section, the frame
call and HYBRID status at every status stride, HYBRID_CENSUS at captures, HYBRID_STOP. Offline tool tools/dev/census_hybrid.py.
Tests: test_lockstep_hybrid.h (arming; owner and non-owner latch, attention split and chain refresh; members, record bytes,
