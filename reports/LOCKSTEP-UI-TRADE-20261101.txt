0. RESULT (default off: native_gate_ui_replay lists 'trade', bit 4096; true does not include it; stop reason 164 as the replay)
  - The trade menus' world changes become events of the client's slot, for EVERY slot, and run on every client at capture tick +
    delay, in slot order and call order, through X4's own functions: buy/sell with the trade menu (AddTradeToShipQueue and the
    docked trade's signal), the money transfers of the economy menus (station and manager accounts), price and stock overrides,
    budgets, the trade menu's virtual cargo mode, and 17 FFI setters of station trade settings.
  - Each slot pays from its own account: X4 keeps the player's money on the player FACTION (section 1), so every replayed call
    of slot k runs with X4's player faction = slot k's faction for the length of the call (the faction scope, section 3). Coop:
    one faction `player`, one shared wallet. Factions mode: x4lan_playerk's own account.
  - Trade offers are no components: they travel as a portable reference (station + ware + buy/sell + mission flag + list +
    place among the matching offers + their count), looked up again at the apply on every client, refused when it no longer
    matches (section 4).
  - Authority: what a call changes must belong to the slot's faction and be no other slot's ship; the slot's own ship trades
    only through the docked trade computer (section 5). Refusals are the same on every client (the world is).
  - The guard leaves the 27 replayed entries to the family when it is armed (covered=4096 in the generated table); what stays
    refused is listed in section 7. With the G4 families plus trade the guard installs 214 entries (241 without trade).

1. THE MONEY MODEL [F]
  - g_PlayerFaction 0x38f4bd0 (U::FactionClass*, written only by FactionDB::Import) is what "the player's money" means:
      Lua GetPlayerMoney 0x288080: [0x38f4bd0]+0x280 -> +0x2e0 (an account handle) -> 0x3178a0 (handle -> account, NULL when none;
        then the dummy account [0x38f4bd8]) -> account vfunc +0x10 = balance in cents (x 0.01 for credits).
      AddPlayerMoney 0x1762d0 -> 0x9dc550 (FactionClass::AddMoney: the same walk, deposit vfunc +0x60) on [0x38f4bd0].
      Lua TransferPlayerMoneyTo 0x2881f0 (amount, container[, reason]) -> 0x9f9be0 / 0x9f9c30 -> 0x3f8430(container, amount,
        [0x38f4bd0], reason): the faction account transfers (AccountClass vfunc +0x70: clamp +0x50, withdraw +0x68, deposit into
        the target +0x60) to the container's account (handle at container+0x700, +0x708 = it is the container's own; without one
        the dummy account). TransferMoneyToPlayer the other way.
      0x9dc7a0 (after every faction money change): if the faction is [0x38f4bd0], a MoneyUpdatedEvent is posted (deferred) on the
        singleton player [[0x3d198f8]+0x230] for MD.
  - The player ENTITY holds no money; it only hears the event. Every faction has its own account (faction+0x280 -> +0x2e0), so
    x4lan_player2 (the pool factions of the identity plan) has one [I: the walk is the same; T2 checks it]. Containers (stations,
    ships) have their own or the dummy account (U::DummyAccount: a transfer out of it creates money, its balance getter draws
    from the thread's RNG).
  - Owner of an object: U::Object::GetTrueOwner = vfunc 708 (+0x1620) = 0x352b10 (mov rax,[rcx+0x348]; ret) for Ship, Station,
    Container and Object alike; TransferPlayerMoneyTo itself compares it with [0x38f4bd0] (0x28840b).
  MONEY DECISION (coordinator default) as built:
    coop      every slot `player`: every slot's trades and transfers use the one player account (shared wallet).
    factions  slot k = x4lan_playerk: slot k's calls run with [0x38f4bd0] = x4lan_playerk, so its money comes from and goes to its
              own faction account; slot 1 keeps `player` (the plan's default). A plan that gives slot 1 a pool faction
              (--occupied pool) gets the same scope for slot 1.
    Slot k's faction is read at the apply as the owner of slot k's ship (every client the same; no ship: nothing runs).

2. WHAT IS REPLAYED (lockstep_ui_trade.h, X4LAN_UI_TRADE at the end of tools/dev/native_gate_ui_replay_core.lua)
  Lua C globals, wrapped by name in the Lua core (the menus look them up at each call; no base menu caches one [F: grep]):
    op 0x58, global number, then the codec's arguments. Subject = the argument the authority rule checks.
    1  AddTradeToShipQueue(offer, ship, amount, immediate[, flag])   subject: ship. menu_map buttonConfirmTrade (buy/sell rows
       of the trade context), menu_interactmenu orderSalvageDeliver. The offer becomes the portable reference (section 4).
    2  TransferPlayerMoneyTo(amount, container[, reason])           subject: container (station/manager accounts: menu_map
       cash transfer, menu_playerinfo, menu_research, menu_station_overview)
    3  TransferMoneyToPlayer(amount, container[, reason])           subject: container (the same menus, station configuration)
    4  SetContainerWarePriceOverride(container, ware, isbuy, price) subject: container (helper.lua, menu_map, station config)
    5  ClearContainerWarePriceOverride(container, ware, isbuy)      subject: container
    6  SetContainerStockLimitOverride(container, ware, limit)       subject: container
    7  ClearContainerStockLimitOverride(container, ware)            subject: container
    8  SetMinBudget(container, credits)                             subject: container
    9  SetMaxBudget(container, credits)                             subject: container
    10 SignalObject(player, 'docked_player_trade_added', ship)      subject: ship. Only this signal; every other SignalObject stays
       the events family's (slot 1) or the guard's. md/orders.xml PlayerTradeOrderAdded creates the trade computer on
       event.param2 and reads nothing of the player [F], so it means the same for every slot; the apply names X4's own player.
    11 SetVirtualCargoMode(container, on[, trades])                 subject: container. The trade context sets it on open and
       clears it on close; it allocates the container's virtual cargo (+0x710) and refreshes a cached time (+0xa18, 0x41a910)
       [F]. Replayed, every client keeps the same state (refused, every trade menu open showed the guard's info line).
  FFI exports, hooked natively at their entries (the guard table's prefixes and pins, looked up by name), op 0x59, hook number,
  then the arguments by spec; the subject is the first argument:
    SetContainerTradeRule (container, TradeRuleID, ruletype, ware or NULL, value)   SetContainerWareIsBuyable / IsSellable
    SetContainerBuyLimitOverride / SellLimitOverride (int32)                        ClearContainerBuyLimitOverride / SellLimitOverride
    AddTradeWare / RemoveTradeWare      SetContainerGlobalPriceFactor / SetContainerBuildPriceFactor (float in xmm1)
    SetContainerBuildMethod             SetContainerWorkforceFillCapacity          SetSupplyManual
    SetShipTradeLoopCargoReservationOverride / RemoveShipTradeLoopCargoReservationOverride (ship)
    UpdateProductionTradeOffers
    Every one is void [F: ffi.cdef], so the capture answers nothing and the menu goes on; TradeRuleIDs travel as numbers: rules
    are only created by CreateTradeRule, which stays refused, so every client has the same rules and IDs.
  Capture: in the replay's local phases (this client's UI), not in the probe's own frame, not during an apply; nothing runs on
    the capturing client before the apply. A value that does not encode (no component, a string over 48 bytes, an offer in no
    list) is refused where it was made: counted, logged (UI_REPLAY_REFUSED / UI_REPLAY_REFUSED_NATIVE), nothing ran anywhere.
  Apply: in the probe's frame of capture tick + delay on every client, slot order, call order (the Lua ones and the FFI ones of
    a slot in the order the menu called them), after the authority check, inside the slot's faction scope (not for the signal:
    MD runs later in the simulation, as slot 1's always did). X4's answers and refusals: UI_REPLAY_APPLIED rows, the receipt.

3. THE FACTION SCOPE (lockstep_ui_trade.h uitrade_scope_enter / uitrade_scope_leave; gate.ui_trade_scope / _scope_end)
  [0x38f4bd0] = the owner of slot k's ship for the length of one stock call, then back. It opens only during an apply
  (uirep_replaying), on the frame thread, with no slot scope (pslot) and no presentation scope open; a second open is refused;
  the close checks the value is still the one it wrote (else stop 164, why=ui_trade_scope, the value put back anyway); a fault
  inside closes it (stop 164, why=ui_trade); a pass that finds it open closes it and stops (ui_trade_scope_open). When slot k's
  faction is `player` (coop, slot 1) nothing is written (scopes=opened/same/refused in UI_TRADE_STATUS).
  What it means: everything the stock function does "for the player" is slot k's faction: whose account pays or is paid,
  whose ownership counts (the stock setters' own player-owned checks), whose logbook money line. The probe's frame runs in the
  UI's VBI slot 6, where the presentation scope already swaps the canonical player's identity; simulation jobs run from slot 11
  on [I: the presentation design's same premise, lockstep_pilot_present.h].
  Known effects, the same on every client: a money change of x4lan_playerk inside the scope posts its MoneyUpdatedEvent on the
  canonical player (0x9dc7a0 compares with [0x38f4bd0]), so MD's player-money listeners (notifications, logbook) hear slot k's
  change as slot 1's.

4. TRADE OFFERS: THE PORTABLE REFERENCE
  At capture (client k): GetTradeData(offer).station; the offer is looked up in GetTradeList(station) (the station's own offers)
  or, for the ware exchange between the trading ship and the station, GetWareExchangeTradeList(ship, station); from its entry:
  ware, buy or sell, mission offer or not; then its place among the entries of that list with the same ware, kind and flag, and
  their count. Wire: {s=<station as a component reference>, w=<ware>, c=kind | 4 mission | 8 exchange | 16*place | 4096*count}.
  At the apply (every client, before the faction scope, in the canonical frame as the capture's list was read): the same list
  of the same station; the matching entries; the count must be the same and the place must exist, else refused offer_changed
  (the same on every client while the economy agrees; a divergent economy is a digest mismatch anyway). The raw offer ID never
  travels (its numbering under runtime IDs is unverified, report 7c).

5. AUTHORITY (Lua, at the apply, every client alike)
  subject ID -> refused slot_ship when it is another slot's ship; no_ship when slot k has none; not_owner when the subject's
  owner (GetComponentData 'owner') is not slot k's ship's owner; AddTradeToShipQueue on slot k's own ship only with immediate
  (the docked trade computer: a person flies it, it takes no trade order; the stock menu offers the occupied ship only docked).
  So: coop, any slot may manage every `player` station, trade with every `player` ship that is no slot ship, and with its own
  ship docked; factions, slot k only with x4lan_playerk's objects. Money transfers only between the slot's faction and a
  container it owns: the stock menus' fees to foreign containers (builder hiring, crew hiring, build plots, ship sale) belong
  to flows that stay refused, so they stay refused too (paying a fee for nothing, or being paid for a ship sale that never
  happened, would be consistent but wrong).

6. NATIVE AND LUA DETAILS
  Wire: the replay's section 'ui' (kind 0x55), version 1, as before; ops 0x58 (Lua) and 0x59 (FFI) are Lua-run (>= 0x40), so the
  native apply in path_release skips them and the probe's frame runs them. Section rules (uirep_event_ok -> uitrade_event_ok):
  the family armed, a Lua global number 1..11, an FFI event whose bytes are exactly its spec (bools 0/1, strings <= 48, NULL
  only where the spec says z). Any slot (each one's faction pays).
  Hooks: 17 five-byte jumps at the FFI entries (prefixes and pins from lockstep_ui_guard_table.h by name: kind FFI, group trade,
  covered by 4096, no RIP-relative byte, no branch in the prefix), a page near the image (strides: prefix, jump back, wrapper),
  installed at the replay's first pass, checked every pass (ui_trade_hook_lost), removed behind the window (ui_trade_restore),
  with uirep_restore on a stop. Pins (fail closed, gate.ui_trade_pins before any patch and again at install): the 17 entries,
  the 10 Lua globals' entries, GetPlayerMoney 0x288080, the handle resolver 0x3178a0, 0x9f9be0, 0x9f9c30, 0x9dc550, AddPlayerMoney,
  GetTrueOwner 0x352b10, and the Ship vtable 0x2b84a98 slot 708 pointing at it.
  Receipt (joins the pilot receipt, so the relay digest): |UITRADE_1:<each slot faction's balance in cents; x without a ship,
  n for a faction without an account>;<slot 1 ship's trade loop override 0/1>;<slot 2 ...>. No container money: a container
  without its own account answers through X4's dummy account, whose balance getter (DummyAccount vfunc 2, 0x96e0a0) draws from
  the thread's RNG [F], and the receipt must not draw; the faction walk reads no dummy either. Rows: UI_TRADE_MONEY when it changes,
  UI_TRADE_STATUS every 120 ticks (native counts: captured refused passed runs skipped scopes=opened/same/refused; Lua
  lua=captured/applied/skipped/refused, view=), UI_TRADE_PINS at the arming, UI_TRADE_FUND, UI_TRADE_VIEW.
  Test fixture native_gate_ui_trade_fund (credits, whole 1..1e9, needs the family): at the fleet tick (window start - 4) every
  client gives every slot faction that is not `player` that much once, by the stock AddPlayerMoney inside that slot's faction
  scope (the pool factions start with nothing to spend). Checked by the probe and check_gate_options.

8. COOP AND FACTIONS: WHAT WORKS WHERE
  Both modes, slots 1..N, scripted (no people) and through the replay: every entry of section 2 with the slot's own faction
  paying and owning (tests: tests/test_native_gate_ui_trade.py ApplyTests in both modes; C: the scope on stand-ins).
  People, coop: the stock menus already show every `player` ship, station and the shared wallet on every client, so the trade
  context, station overview, station configuration, player info's cash transfers all reach the family.
  People, factions mode, client k (k >= 2):
    works [I: built, not live]: the docked trade with the own ship (map or docked menu -> station -> Trade): the trade view, this
      client's UI only and reads only, adds the own slot ship to GetTradeShipList's answer and answers GetPlayerMoney with
      x4lan_playerk's balance (so the menu prices against the right wallet; the top bar shows it too);
    does NOT work, and why: managing x4lan_playerk's stations and non-slot ships through stock menus. The stock menus decide
      "own" by the owner being [0x38f4bd0] (`player`), on client k too: x4lan_playerk's stations open as foreign stations
      without controls. The replay supports them (scripted ops, the scope, the authority rule); the missing piece is a faction
      view of the whole presentation ([0x38f4bd0] = x4lan_playerk inside client k's presentation scope). Not built: it would also
      move map knowledge ("known to" is per faction: x4lan_playerk starts knowing nothing), relations, the logbook and every
      other `player` check of the HUD to x4lan_playerk at once. Also: the identity plans give factions mode no non-slot ships,
      and --ui-replay-fleet is coop only (builder), so trade orders for fleet ships are coop-only in practice.
  Both: NPC trader inventory and trade rule creation (section 7).

9. RISKS AND UNKNOWNS
  [I] the scope's premise: no simulation job reads [0x38f4bd0] while the probe's frame runs (UI slot 6); the presentation scope
      relies on the same.
  [I] who pays when a trade ORDER executes later in the simulation (outside any scope): the engine's own logic for the ship's
      owner; for x4lan_playerk's ships unverified (T3 checks it). The immediate trade computer (md/orders.xml create_trade_computer)
      on an x4lan_playerk ship is unverified too.
  [F] the trade menu's queries refresh caches on the client that runs them: GetCargoAfterTradeOrders 0x2b1350 creates and frees
      a temporary virtual cargo through 0x3ff3a0 -> 0x41a910, which writes container+0xa18 (a time). A query is not guarded; if
      the simulation reads +0xa18 before its own refresh, a menu open on one client could part the world [I]. The census / world
      hash is the check; SetVirtualCargoMode itself is replayed now.
  Known, the same everywhere: MoneyUpdatedEvent of slot k's money on slot 1's player entity (section 3); the capturing menu shows
      the old money, cargo and settings until the apply (delay ticks), so a second click is a second trade; a menu that reads a
      setter's result back in the same frame shows the old value for a frame.
  Registration lines (for the merge with the parallel families): UIREP_FAMILY_TRADE=4096 (lockstep_pilot_inputs.h; 1024 and
      2048 left to the others), ops 0x58/0x59 (lockstep_ui_replay.h), the family list in K.FAMILY, check_gate_options.py,
      native_gate_players.py; the guard inventory's covered bits; one line each in uirep_event_ok, uirep_op_name, uirep_guard_pass,
      uirep_restore, lua_ui_replay_push, the core's session/run/frame/script, the probe's pins, fund and local_mark; METHOD and
      includes in lockstep_gate.c / test_lockstep_gate.c; the replay C test's "every hook" excludes trade (its own test).
