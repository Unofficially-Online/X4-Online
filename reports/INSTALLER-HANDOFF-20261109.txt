Installer guidance
- The owner's short responsiveness test was visually playable with 120 rolling
  census slices and status stride 60. The earlier full census every 60 ticks
  caused periodic pauses of approximately 0.42 seconds. A rolling census is
  diagnostic sampling across different ticks, not an atomic universe proof.
- Do not enable the experimental SIGTRAP input/IK observers in a normal player
  installer. Their lifecycle/coverage limitations are documented in the reports.
- This branch does not establish a complete native Linux player client or a
  successful remote Windows/Linux human playtest.
